# Shipping Policy

## Where we ship
SneakerHut ships to all 50 US states and to Canada.
We do not ship to other countries at this time.

## Shipping options (US)
- Standard shipping: 5-7 business days, $6.99
- Express shipping: 2-3 business days, $14.99
- Free standard shipping on orders over $100

## Shipping to Canada
- Standard: 7-12 business days, $19.99
- Customers are responsible for any customs fees or duties.

## Processing time
Orders placed before 2 PM Central Time on business days ship the same day.
Orders placed on weekends or holidays ship the next business day.

## Tracking
A tracking number is emailed once your order ships.
You can also check your order status by giving us your order number.
